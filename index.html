Research Hypothesis

Falsifiable Research Statement

ISEF scoring allocates critical weight to whether a clear, testable hypothesis exists. Ours is:

Primary Hypothesis

If graded-Z composite shielding layers incorporating high-Z elements (Bi, La) identified through autonomous thermodynamic screening are compared against aerospace-standard Aluminum 6061-T6, then those composites will demonstrate a ≥ 15% improvement in mass attenuation efficiency (cm²/g) across the 0.1–10 MeV gamma energy spectrum, as verified by OpenMC Monte Carlo transport simulations calibrated within 5% of NIST XCOM reference values.

Independent Variable

Elemental composition and mass fractions of the composite material (HDPE %, Bi₂O₃ %, B₄C %), generated via NSGA-II genetic algorithm and screened by thermodynamic convex hull stability (ΔE_hull ≤ 0.05 eV/atom).

Dependent Variable

Mass attenuation coefficient (μ/ρ, cm²/g) and Shielding Efficiency Score (SES) computed from ensemble ML predictions and validated by OpenMC fixed-source photon transport with ENDF/B-VIII.0 cross-section libraries.
